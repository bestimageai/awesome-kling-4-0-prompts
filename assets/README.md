# Media sources

The logo is the existing bestimage.ai brand asset used in this organization’s video libraries.

Official previews, references, and videos are linked from Kling AI’s own hosting. They were published in the [official user guide](https://kling.ai/quickstart/kling-4-model-user-guide); their original rights remain with their respective owners. They are not generated by bestimage.ai or licensed under this repository’s MIT license. No official media has been edited or rehosted here.

The [example gallery](../docs/official-examples.md) preserves the source grouping and links to full original prompts. Every practice link points to a separate creative exercise.
