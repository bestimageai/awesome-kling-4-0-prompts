# Omni Reference direction

[Catalog](README.md) · [Official examples](../docs/official-examples.md)

Supply your own authorized assets and match the numbered references to the upload order. Each asset has a limited role. These recipes use Omni Reference, not the separate Keyframes workflow.

## K073 — Two makers at one bench

**Use:** multi-person identity direction. **Mode:** Omni Reference. **Brief:** 12 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** two adult portraits and a workshop image.

```text
@Image1 defines the maker standing left. @Image2 defines the maker standing right. Preserve their separate faces, hairstyles, and clothing. @Image3 defines the workshop layout and light. Place one plain wooden frame on the bench between them. The left maker holds the frame upright while the right maker aligns a loose corner piece, then both step back to inspect it. Use a slow lateral camera move that keeps both faces and the frame visible. Hear wood contact, sleeves, and quiet workshop ambience. Keep the same two people throughout. No dialogue, subtitles, or music.
```

**Adjust:** portraits and workshop. **Review:** two distinct identities; hand roles remain fixed; frame geometry survives occlusion.

## K074 — Product in a new room

**Use:** placing an owned product in a campaign environment. **Mode:** Omni Reference. **Brief:** 10 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** product photo and empty-room image.

```text
@Image1 defines only the shape, surface, and proportions of the small wooden radio. @Image2 defines the room, side table, and window light. Place one radio on the side table with its front facing the camera. Begin in a wide room view and slowly move closer until the radio fills the center third of the frame. Keep its controls, speaker grille, and edges unchanged. Match its contact shadow to the room's existing light. Sound: quiet room tone and distant wind outside. The radio stays silent. No text overlays, hands, dialogue, or music.
```

**Adjust:** room reference, framing. **Review:** product reference does not replace room; grille count and table contact remain stable.

## K076 — Camera path around a sculpture

**Use:** transfer a camera move without copying its subject. **Mode:** Omni Reference. **Brief:** 12 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** sculpture image, courtyard image, 12-second camera-reference clip.

```text
@Image1 defines the single abstract stone sculpture. @Image2 defines the empty courtyard and daylight. Use @Video1 only for the camera's slow quarter-orbit and final distance. Place the sculpture on the courtyard's central plinth. Follow the reference camera path while keeping the sculpture stationary and its silhouette, holes, and stone grain consistent. The background should reveal connected courtyard surfaces as the view changes. Hear soft outdoor ambience and distant leaves. End at the reference move's stopping point. No people, new objects, text, speech, or music.
```

**Adjust:** sculpture and courtyard. **Review:** orbit belongs to camera; holes reveal the changing background correctly.

```text
@Image1 defines the adult's face, hair, and body proportions. @Image2 defines only the long coat's cut, fabric, buttons, and color. Show the adult wearing that coat in a plain dressing room. No dialogue, captions, music, or outfit change during the shot.
```

## K078 — The same cup, two views

**Use:** product consistency across shots. **Mode:** Omni Reference. **Brief:** 12 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** one cup reference and a tabletop reference.

```text
@Image1 defines one pale blue ceramic cup, including its handle and glaze markings. @Image2 defines the tabletop and soft window lighting. Shot one lasts six seconds: a close side view as a hand turns the cup slightly so its handle points right. Cut to an overhead view for six seconds, beginning with that same handle orientation and hand position. The hand leaves and the camera holds. Keep the cup empty and preserve the glaze markings across the cut. Sound: ceramic sliding softly, then room tone. No text, dialogue, or music.
```

**Adjust:** cup reference, shot lengths. **Review:** handle orientation bridges the cut; glaze marks do not change sides.

## K079 — Storyboard to finished puppet scene

**Use:** storyboard-led animation. **Mode:** Omni Reference. **Brief:** 15 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** original puppet design and a three-panel storyboard.

```text
@Image1 defines the original wooden puppet's design and painted colors. @Image2 defines three shots: the puppet approaches a closed garden gate, opens the latch, then looks into the garden. Render finished miniature animation rather than a view of the storyboard. Give each panel five seconds, preserving its framing and left-to-right travel. The latch moves only when the puppet's hand touches it, and the gate swings away from the puppet. Keep warm morning light consistent. Hear wooden footsteps, a latch click, and garden birds. No panel borders, annotations, subtitles, speech, or music.
```

**Adjust:** character design and storyboard. **Review:** panel order respected; output is full-frame animation; gate contact remains believable.

## K080 — Surface reference only

**Use:** material direction without shape transfer. **Mode:** Omni Reference. **Brief:** 8 seconds, 1:1. **Inputs:** chair shape reference and close-up material swatch.

```text
@Image1 defines the exact shape of one simple chair: its legs, seat, and backrest. @Image2 defines only the warm textured upholstery material. Apply that material to the seat and backrest while retaining the wooden legs from @Image1. Place the chair on a neutral studio floor. Move the camera slowly from a frontal view to a shallow three-quarter view. Preserve the chair's proportions and four floor contacts. Hear quiet studio ambience. Keep the upholstery pattern anchored to its surfaces. No people, text, dialogue, or music.
```

**Adjust:** swatch and studio backdrop. **Review:** swatch does not redefine chair shape; fabric follows perspective; four legs remain.

## K081 — A controlled background cast

**Use:** foreground identity with background activity. **Mode:** Omni Reference. **Brief:** 12 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** authorized adult portrait and market-location image.

```text
@Image1 defines the adult florist in the foreground. @Image2 defines the market aisle and awnings, without transferring identifiable people. Show the florist tying a plain ribbon around one small bouquet while two distant, softly focused shoppers walk across the aisle behind them. Keep focus on the florist's face and hands. The camera stays fixed at counter height. Preserve the florist's clothing and the bouquet's size. Hear ribbon, faint footsteps, and indistinct market ambience. End as the florist sets the bouquet down. No dialogue, readable signs, subtitles, or music.
```

**Adjust:** bouquet colors, location reference. **Review:** background figures stay separate; focus stays foreground; ribbon remains attached to bouquet.
