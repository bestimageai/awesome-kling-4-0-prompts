# Action and camera movement

[Catalog](README.md) · [Official camera example](../docs/official-examples.md#o03)

Separate the subject's route from the camera's route. Each recipe has a visible start, contact event, and stopping point.

<a id="k037"></a>
## K037 — The rolling orange

**Use:** a low-angle tracking exercise. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 8 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
One orange rolls from left to right across a wooden kitchen table after an adult hand gives it a gentle push. Track alongside it at table height, keeping its textured peel sharp while the kitchen behind it blurs. A folded cloth lies across the route. The orange reaches the cloth, slows, and stops against its first fold. The camera decelerates with it and holds. Hear peel brushing wood and a soft cloth contact. Keep the orange round, the table level, and the cloth stationary. No dialogue, subtitles, music, or cut.
```

**Adjust:** table length, cloth color. **Review:** rotation agrees with travel; slowing starts at contact.

## K038 — A skater's gentle turn

**Use:** controlled sports motion. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 12 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
An adult roller skater wearing a helmet and pads glides slowly through an empty outdoor practice court. Begin in a wide side view. The skater bends their knees, follows a broad painted curve to the left, and coasts to a steady two-foot stop. Track parallel at a constant safe distance, ending before the skater reaches the frame edge. Late-afternoon shadows remain attached to the wheels. Hear wheels, clothing, and a gentle stop. Keep the same skates and protective gear throughout. No jumps, other skaters, dialogue, captions, or music.
```

**Adjust:** court colors, wardrobe. **Review:** wheels contact ground; lean matches the turn; camera and subject slow separately.

## K039 — Shuttle landing

**Use:** a sports detail cutaway. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 8 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
At floor level on an indoor badminton court, follow one shuttlecock descending into an empty service box. It lands cork-first, tips onto its feather skirt, and rocks once before settling. Make a short downward tilt to follow the landing, then hold with the court line visible behind it. The feather structure stays intact and the cork remains attached. Soft overhead light casts a compact shadow that meets the shuttle at impact. Sound: one light floor tap and a short feather rustle, with distant indoor ambience. No racket, people entering, speech, or music.
```

**Adjust:** framing, court surface. **Review:** shadow converges before impact; bounce is small; feathers preserve their structure.

## K040 — Crossing behind a pillar

**Use:** continuity through occlusion. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 10 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
An adult in a red coat walks left to right through a covered arcade carrying one small blue bag in their right hand. Track parallel from the opposite side of a single broad stone pillar. The pillar briefly hides the person halfway through the shot; they reappear on its right at the same walking speed. Keep their coat, face, bag, and hand assignment unchanged. End as they stop beside the next arch and look outward. Hear evenly spaced footsteps continuing during the occlusion. Use soft overcast light. No cuts, dialogue, captions, or music.
```

**Adjust:** coat and bag colors. **Review:** stride continues behind the pillar; reappearance position matches elapsed time.

## K041 — The sweeping fabric

**Use:** dance-camera coordination. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 12 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
One adult dancer stands in a bright studio holding a long blue fabric strip at both ends. They raise it in a shallow arc, turn half a rotation, and lower it until its center touches the floor. The camera makes a slow quarter-circle around the dancer in the opposite direction, keeping the whole body visible. The fabric trails the hands and settles after the dancer stops. Hear bare feet, cloth moving through air, and quiet breathing. Preserve one dancer, two grip points, and one continuous strip. No music, spoken words, subtitles, or cuts.
```

**Adjust:** fabric length and color. **Review:** cloth follows momentum; feet bear weight; the orbit never crops the hands.

## K042 — The ferry rope

**Use:** weight and tension detail. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 10 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
Close view of a thick mooring rope already secured around a dock bollard. A small boat beyond the frame drifts gently, causing the rope to tighten, lift slightly from the wet dock, then relax back into its original curve. Keep the camera locked and the knot fixed. Gray harbor light reveals wet fibers and the worn metal bollard. Hear water against the dock, a faint rope creak, and one distant gull. The rope remains a continuous strand and the tension change travels along it. No people, captions, dialogue, or music.
```

**Adjust:** rope color, dock material. **Review:** fixed knot; continuous fibers; lift corresponds to increasing tension.

## K043 — A paper glider's arrival

**Use:** readable airborne movement. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 8 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
One plain cream paper glider enters a quiet room from the left, glides diagonally toward a broad desk, and lands with its nose touching an open notebook. Pan gently right to follow its route without moving closer. The glider dips gradually and its shadow crosses the desk before contact. End with the wings resting on the desktop and the nose against the notebook edge. Hear a light air swish and paper landing. Preserve the folded wing shape and room layout. No hand entering, readable writing, dialogue, or music.
```

**Adjust:** paper color, room palette. **Review:** flight loses height smoothly; contact stops forward motion; wings remain connected.

## K044 — The stair landing reveal

**Use:** an architectural camera reveal. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 12 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
Begin at the bottom of a wide indoor staircase, looking upward along its left handrail. Move the camera slowly up the stairs at walking height. As it reaches the landing, reveal one large round window and a potted olive tree beside it. Keep the movement continuous, with slight human walking rhythm rather than a floating rise through the steps. End level with the landing and hold the window composition. Morning light casts consistent rail shadows. Sound: measured footsteps and soft interior ambience. No people, text, dialogue, or music.
```

**Adjust:** stair materials, window light. **Review:** camera follows the stairs; rail geometry stays continuous; landing exists before arrival.

## K045 — A slow cart stop

**Use:** inertia in an everyday scene. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 10 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
An adult museum worker pushes a small cart carrying three folded fabric rolls along a flat corridor. Track beside the cart at wheel height. The worker slows before a doorway, and the rolls shift slightly forward together before settling against the cart's raised front edge. The cart stops fully and the worker keeps both hands on the handle. Use even daylight and quiet colors. Hear rolling wheels, fabric settling, and the final wheel squeak. Keep all three rolls on the cart and preserve its four wheels. No sudden collision, dialogue, subtitles, or music.
```

**Adjust:** roll colors, corridor. **Review:** load reacts after deceleration; wheels stop rotating; hands stay on the handle.
