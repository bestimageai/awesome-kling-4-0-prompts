# Cinematic stories

[Catalog](README.md) · [Prompting guide](../docs/prompting-guide.md) · [Kling 4.0 Text to Video](https://bestimage.ai/models/kuaishou/kling-4-0-text-to-video/)

Small stories told through a visible decision, a physical action, and a readable ending. These are creative recipes; durations and framing describe the intended shot.

<a id="k001"></a>
## K001 — The returned umbrella

**Use:** a neighborhood-business story. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 10 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
A continuous ten-second medium-wide shot inside a small umbrella repair shop after rain. An adult shopkeeper in a dark green apron stands behind a wooden counter. One closed yellow umbrella lies horizontally on the counter with its curved handle pointing right. During the first three seconds, the shopkeeper slides a folded blue cloth beneath the umbrella. From three to seven seconds, they slowly rotate the closed umbrella a quarter turn so the repaired seam faces the customer. During the final three seconds, they withdraw both hands and smile gently toward the customer just outside the frame. The camera moves slightly closer without changing sides. Soft window light reveals wet pavement outside and the matte yellow fabric inside. Sound: quiet rain outside, cloth brushing wood, and one small handle tap. No dialogue, subtitles, or background music. Keep the umbrella closed, with the same handle, fabric color, and position on the counter.
```

**Adjust:** umbrella color, apron color, window light. **Review:** one umbrella remains closed; the cloth stays beneath it; the seam rotates with the fabric; hands release before the final hold.

## K002 — The late rehearsal

**Use:** a quiet short-film opening. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 12 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
In an empty rehearsal room, an adult cellist in a gray sweater sits beside one closed cello case. Begin with a static wide view of the musician, case, and a spare chair. For four seconds, the musician listens to rain tapping the high window. They then move the spare chair slightly closer to their own and lay a folded scarf across its back. Finish with their hand resting on the scarf while they look toward the closed door. Make a gentle forward camera move only after the chair stops. Cool window light and one warm wall lamp remain constant. Hear rain, chair feet on wood, and the soft scarf movement. No dialogue or music. The cello case stays closed and the room remains empty apart from the musician.
```

**Adjust:** instrument case, scarf, room palette. **Review:** the spare chair moves once; the case stays fixed; the eyeline ends at the door.

## K003 — The last parcel

**Use:** an end-of-day character beat. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 10 seconds, 9:16. **Inputs:** none.

```text
Frame an adult bicycle courier at a sheltered apartment entrance, waist up, with a small brown parcel under their left arm. The courier reaches for the doorbell with their right hand, pauses, and notices a sleeping dog through the glass. They lower that hand, set the parcel gently on a dry bench beside the door, and step backward out of the right edge of the frame. Keep the camera locked and the glass reflections subdued so both the pause and the sleeping dog are legible. Evening light falls from the left. Sound: distant traffic, cardboard touching wood, and a quiet shoe scuff. The dog stays asleep. No speech, subtitles, or music.
```

**Adjust:** coat color, entrance materials. **Review:** parcel transfers from arm to bench; the courier never presses the bell; glass geometry remains stable.

## K004 — A seat in the sunlight

**Use:** a gentle hospitality story. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 12 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
One adult café worker stands inside a sunlit window beside two empty chairs and a small round table. In a continuous eye-level shot, the worker notices the narrow sunlight falling between the chairs, moves the left chair into the light, then places a plain ceramic cup on the table nearest that chair. The worker steps aside and looks toward someone approaching outside the frame. Let the camera slide a short distance right while maintaining the table between the chairs. End with the empty sunlit seat and cup prominent. Hear chair legs, a ceramic tap, and distant café conversation without intelligible words. Keep the sunlight direction, furniture count, and cup shape unchanged. No added titles or music.
```

**Adjust:** chair material, cup color. **Review:** table remains still; chair lands in the existing light; the final view invites a person to sit.

## K005 — The stage door ribbon

**Use:** a backstage suspense beat. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 15 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
An adult stagehand in black work clothes waits beside a closed theater door. A narrow red ribbon protrudes beneath it. Start low on the ribbon, tilt slowly to the stagehand noticing it, then remain at waist height as they crouch. They draw only the loose end clear of the doorway and tie it around their wrist. A warm strip of light appears beneath the door as the sound of applause begins on the other side. Finish on the stagehand's small relieved smile, with the door still closed. Keep the corridor dim and the red ribbon continuous. Sound: fabric dragging, a distant stage cue, then muffled applause. No spoken lines, subtitles, or musical score.
```

**Adjust:** ribbon color, corridor lighting. **Review:** ribbon has a continuous origin and path; the door stays shut; applause arrives after the light.

## K006 — Two reflections

**Use:** restrained visual storytelling. **Mode:** image to video. **Brief:** 8 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** a first frame showing one adult beside a rain-streaked shop window.

```text
Animate the supplied first frame as one eight-second shot. Keep the same adult, coat, window mullions, and reflected street. The person slowly wipes a small oval of condensation with their sleeve, revealing a clearer reflection of their own face. They lean closer, straighten their collar, then relax their shoulders. The camera stays on the original side of the window and moves only slightly toward the cleared area. Reflected traffic continues along the street behind them. Hear sleeve friction, light rain, and distant tires. Keep the reflection synchronized with the person's gestures and retain moisture outside the wiped oval. No second person, dialogue, subtitles, or music.
```

**Adjust:** first-frame clothing and weather. **Review:** reflection follows real movement; wiped area grows from sleeve contact; window lines remain straight.

## K007 — The borrowed spotlight

**Use:** a small act of generosity. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 15 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
Inside a community exhibition, an adult painter notices that their own landscape is brightly lit while a neighboring small sculpture is in shadow. Start in a medium-wide view showing the painter, both artworks, and a low adjustable display lamp. The painter turns the lamp toward the sculpture, watches its textured surface become visible, and steps back beside another visitor. The camera pans gently with the beam, ending on the sculpture with the painter softly visible behind it. Both artworks remain fixed. Sound: quiet room tone, the lamp joint creaking, and footsteps. The change comes from the lamp's direction rather than an overall exposure shift. No readable labels, dialogue, or score.
```

**Adjust:** artwork medium, lamp finish. **Review:** beam changes direction continuously; background exposure remains stable; two people remain distinct.

## K008 — The ticket in the book

**Use:** a memory-driven close-up. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 10 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
A pair of adult hands opens a worn blue hardback on a kitchen table. One faded, unprinted orange ticket slips from between the pages and lands face up. Hold close enough to read the paper texture but not any text. The right hand stops turning pages, lifts the ticket by one corner, then places it carefully beside a small framed photograph facing away from the camera. Slowly pull focus from the open book to the ticket as it settles. Morning light remains soft and neutral. Hear page turns, paper landing, and a quiet kettle in another room. End with both hands withdrawn. Keep the book, ticket, and frame in their original positions after placement.
```

**Adjust:** book and ticket colors. **Review:** exactly one ticket emerges; paper bends at the grip; the focus change follows the action.

## K009 — Closing the flower stall

**Use:** a warm closing shot. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 15 seconds, 9:16. **Inputs:** none.

```text
At dusk, an adult flower seller draws a canvas awning halfway down over a small street stall. One white flower remains in a glass jar on the counter. The seller pauses, takes the jar, and places it on the outside ledge where passing people can still see it. They finish lowering the awning and switch off the lamp behind it. Keep a fixed frontal composition with the jar in the lower foreground. The flower stays illuminated by the neighboring shop's warm spill light as the stall darkens. Sound: canvas rolling, glass touching stone, one switch click, and evening street ambience. No dialogue, subtitles, or music.
```

**Adjust:** flower variety, awning color. **Review:** the jar travels visibly from counter to ledge; the final flower remains outside; illumination has a consistent source.
