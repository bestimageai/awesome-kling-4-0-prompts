# Design, explanation, and production studies

[Catalog](README.md) · [Prompting guide](../docs/prompting-guide.md)

Keep explanatory scenes visually inspectable. Abstract diagrams and staging studies are illustrative, not measurements or engineering instructions.

<a id="k100"></a>
## K100 — Three shapes find their spaces

**Use:** a simple shape-matching animation. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 12 seconds, 1:1. **Inputs:** none.

```text
On a matte cream background, a blue circle, red square, and yellow triangle sit below three matching outlined spaces. In a locked overhead view, move the circle straight into the round outline, then the square into the square outline, then the triangle into the triangular outline. Pause briefly after each fit. Keep every shape rigid, flat, and constant in size. Use soft contact shadows and a gentle wooden tap for each arrival. Finish with all three neatly aligned. No labels, numbers, spoken lesson, or music.
```

**Adjust:** colors and spacing. **Review:** one shape moves at a time; outline geometry matches; no resizing to force a fit.

## K101 — Shadow length study

**Use:** a light-position illustration. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 12 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
A small wooden cylinder stands on a plain tabletop with one visible desk lamp to its left. In a fixed wide close-up, the lamp head moves slowly higher on its articulated arm while remaining aimed at the cylinder. Show the cylinder's cast shadow becoming shorter as the light rises. Keep the cylinder and camera still, and retain a visible connection between lamp joints. Hear a faint joint creak and room ambience. Finish with the lamp held in its raised position. No text, extra light sources, dialogue, or music.
```

**Adjust:** object height and lamp finish. **Review:** shadow changes with light position; object stays fixed; joints remain connected.

## K102 — A balance of blocks

**Use:** an illustrative weight-balance scene. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 10 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
A simple wooden balance beam rests on a central triangular support. One identical blue block sits near each end, keeping it level. An adult hand slides the right block slowly toward the center; the left end lowers gradually while the right end rises. Keep the camera fixed at beam height so the pivot and both blocks remain visible. Hear wood sliding and a small creak. The blocks remain on the beam and the support stays fixed. End with the tilted arrangement still. No labels, formulas, speech, or music.
```

**Adjust:** block color, beam length. **Review:** tilt responds to changed lever arm; blocks retain size; pivot does not move.

## K103 — Color through clear sheets

**Use:** a visual layering demonstration. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 10 seconds, 1:1. **Inputs:** none.

```text
Two translucent sheets, one cyan and one yellow, lie partly separated on a white light table. Adult fingertips slide the yellow sheet over the cyan sheet, creating a green overlap. Pause at half overlap, then slide it back to the original position. Keep a fixed overhead camera and constant light-table brightness. Show the sheet edges and slight surface reflections. Hear thin plastic moving. The green region should exist only where the sheets overlap. No printed diagrams, captions, dialogue, or music.
```

**Adjust:** sheet shapes and overlap distance. **Review:** overlap color follows geometry; uncovered colors remain stable.

## K104 — A visual rhythm of tiles

**Use:** abstract motion graphics. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 8 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
Five flat blue square tiles form a horizontal row on a pale background. Each tile rises slightly and returns to its place, one after another from left to right, like a slow wave. Keep their sizes, spacing, and orientation fixed. Use a locked frontal camera and soft shadows directly below each tile. Play one gentle wooden note at the lowest contact point of each return. End with all five aligned and still. No letters, logos, additional tiles, voiceover, or backing music.
```

**Adjust:** tile color, timing between rises. **Review:** sequence has five beats; shadows follow height; row returns to its original arrangement.

<a id="k105"></a>
## K105 — Clay blocks for camera blocking

**Use:** simple scene previsualization. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 12 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
Create a plain gray miniature room with one doorway, a table, and two simple featureless clay figures. Figure A begins beside the doorway; figure B stands at the far side of the table. A walks to the near side, stops, and gestures toward B. Move the camera slowly from the doorway to a clear two-shot without crossing the line between the figures. Keep the figures as simple forms and the set untextured. Sound is limited to light footsteps. No faces, dialogue, text, or music.
```

**Adjust:** table and doorway positions before generation. **Review:** figures remain on opposite table sides; eyeline axis consistent; no surface detail distracts.

## K106 — A clean turn for compositing

**Use:** a simple compositing element. **Mode:** image to video. **Brief:** 8 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** first frame of an owned toy on an evenly lit green backdrop.

```text
Keep the toy design and uniform green background from the supplied frame. Rotate the toy slowly through one quarter turn around its vertical center, as if on a concealed turntable. Keep its base at the same screen position and its entire silhouette inside the frame. Use even soft light with minimal green spill and a restrained contact shadow. End with a two-second hold. Sound: quiet studio ambience. Preserve all appendages and surface details. No camera movement, new props, text, speech, or music.
```

**Adjust:** rotation direction and toy reference. **Review:** silhouette stays complete; backdrop remains uniform; base does not drift.

## K107 — The empty title space

**Use:** a background plate for later typography. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 10 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
A soft cream fabric ribbon drifts slowly along the bottom quarter of a pale blue studio background. It enters from the left, forms one broad curve, and settles toward the right without crossing the upper three quarters. Keep the camera locked and the background evenly lit. Show subtle fabric weave and a soft shadow near the lower edge. Sound: a faint cloth swish that fades. Finish with the ribbon nearly still. Keep the upper space empty for later composition. No generated lettering, logos, people, dialogue, or music.
```

**Adjust:** palette and ribbon width. **Review:** reserved area remains clear; ribbon stays continuous; no accidental letter shapes.

## K108 — A focus handoff

**Use:** a controlled focus exercise. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 10 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
Two small wooden houses stand on a tabletop, one near the lens on the left and one farther away on the right. Begin with the near house sharp and the far house softly blurred. Over four seconds, pull focus smoothly to the far house while keeping the camera and both objects stationary. Hold for two seconds, then return focus gently to the near house. Soft side light reveals wood grain and stable shadows. Hear quiet room ambience. Preserve each house's roof and window shapes. No object movement, text, dialogue, or music.
```

**Adjust:** distance between houses, focus speed. **Review:** only focus changes; framing and scale stay fixed; no geometry morphing.
