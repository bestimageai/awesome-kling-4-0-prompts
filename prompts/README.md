## Choose by starting material

- A written idea: begin with [cinematic stories](cinematic-stories.md), [creator clips](creator-social.md), or [design studies](design-education.md).
- An approved first image: choose an image-to-video recipe in [product films](product-films.md) or [nature and spaces](nature-spaces.md).
- Several assets with different roles: use [Omni Reference](omni-reference.md).
- Existing footage to revise: use [bounded video edits](video-editing.md).
- Required opening, ending, or intermediate compositions: use [keyframes](keyframe-transitions.md).
