# Nature, travel, and spaces

[Catalog](README.md) · [Image-led workflow](../docs/bestimage-workflow.md)

Atmosphere comes from a concrete change in light, air, water, or movement. Nature scenes are creative depictions.

## K064 — Reed reflection

**Use:** a quiet nature insert. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 10 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
Three tall reeds stand at the edge of a still pond in soft morning light. Frame their lower stems and reflections, with the bank along the left edge. A small ripple enters from the right, passes through the reflections, and fades while the actual stems remain nearly still. Hold the camera fixed and keep the water level constant. Hear distant birds and a faint breeze. Finish with the reflections becoming clear again. Preserve all three stems and their corresponding reflected positions. No animals entering, text, speech, or music.
```

**Adjust:** light warmth, ripple size. **Review:** only reflections distort; wave direction remains consistent; stems stay rooted.

## K065 — The curtain and the courtyard

**Use:** hospitality atmosphere. **Mode:** image to video. **Brief:** 12 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** first frame of an open guest-room window facing a courtyard.

```text
Animate the supplied room without changing its architecture or furniture. A light curtain billows gently inward, briefly reveals more of the courtyard, then settles beside the open window. Move the camera slowly toward the window while keeping the sill horizontal and the courtyard perspective continuous. Sunlight remains outside, with soft reflected light inside. Hear leaves, distant birds, and the curtain brushing the wall. End with a clear view through the opening. The window frame stays fixed. No people, text, dialogue, or music.
```

**Adjust:** curtain fabric, time of day. **Review:** curtain remains attached; courtyard scale changes with camera distance; geometry stays stable.

## K066 — Wet stones after rain

**Use:** a destination mood shot. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 10 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
Move slowly along a low stone wall beside a village path just after rain. Start close to wet stone texture, then let the view open toward a small garden gate at the end of the wall. Water beads cling to moss and one drop falls into a shallow puddle below. Keep the camera at the same height, following the wall rather than passing through it. Soft gray sky reflects in the puddle. Hear distant birds, a single drop, and light wind. No people, signs, dialogue, or music.
```

**Adjust:** stone color, garden plants. **Review:** wall connects continuously to gate; drop falls from a visible source.

## K067 — The resting dog portrait

**Use:** a calm pet profile. **Mode:** image to video. **Brief:** 8 seconds, 9:16. **Inputs:** authorized first frame of one dog lying on a mat.

```text
Keep the dog, mat, room, and camera position from the supplied image. The dog lifts its head slightly at a soft sound off-camera, turns one ear toward it, then rests its chin back on the mat. Preserve the coat markings, muzzle length, collar, and body proportions. Use gentle breathing and a natural blink without moving the legs. Sound: a faint off-camera fabric rustle and quiet room ambience. End in a relaxed pose close to the opening composition. No human speech, subtitles, music, or additional animals.
```

**Adjust:** sound direction, lighting. **Review:** markings remain fixed; head returns naturally; dog stays relaxed.

## K068 — Footbridge morning

**Use:** a slow travel establishing shot. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 15 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
Begin on a wooden footbridge above a narrow stream, facing the far bank. Move forward slowly down its center until a sunlit meadow becomes visible beyond the last post. Keep both rails continuous and the stream flowing in the same direction below. A few dry leaves rest on the boards and remain in place as the camera passes. Hear water, measured footsteps, and distant birds. End before leaving the bridge, with the meadow framed by the rails. No people, signs, dialogue, or music.
```

**Adjust:** wood finish, meadow colors. **Review:** rail posts pass at plausible intervals; camera stays above the deck.

## K069 — The leaf shadow clock

**Use:** a minimal interior ambience clip. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 10 seconds, 1:1. **Inputs:** none.

```text
A plain terracotta pot stands near a white wall in afternoon sunlight. The plant itself is mostly above the frame; its leaf shadows move slowly across the wall as a breeze stirs the branches. Keep the pot and camera completely still. One long shadow crosses the pot's rim, then returns toward the wall. Preserve soft penumbra and the warm reflected light on the clay. Hear faint leaves and outdoor ambience. No visible clock, text, people, speech, or music. End with the shadows still moving gently.
```

**Adjust:** plant silhouette, wall color. **Review:** shadows respond as a connected plant; pot remains grounded.

## K070 — A snowflake on wool

**Use:** a seasonal macro insert. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 8 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
Extreme close-up of a dark wool scarf outdoors. One small snowflake rests across two visible fibers. It softens gradually into a clear bead that sinks slightly into the weave. Keep the camera fixed and the background softly gray. A faint breeze moves only the loose fiber tips. Hear quiet winter ambience without amplified melting sounds. Preserve the same patch of fabric throughout and let the change remain small enough to inspect. No hands, text, speech, music, or additional falling snow.
```

**Adjust:** wool color, magnification. **Review:** one flake becomes one bead; fibers stay continuous; scale remains consistent.

## K071 — Gallery corner reveal

**Use:** a property walkthrough detail. **Mode:** image to video. **Brief:** 12 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** first frame of an empty gallery corridor leading around a corner.

```text
Move the camera slowly forward through the supplied corridor, then turn right around the visible corner to reveal a small seating alcove with one bench. Preserve the existing wall thickness, floor pattern, ceiling height, and light direction. Keep the movement at eye level, easing through the turn and stopping before the bench. Sound: soft footsteps and quiet room reverberation. The reveal should feel like entering connected space rather than changing rooms through a dissolve. No people, labels, captions, dialogue, or music.
```

**Adjust:** bench material, corridor palette. **Review:** floor pattern continues around the corner; bench appears through occlusion.

## K072 — The tide reaches a shell

**Use:** a coastal texture shot. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 10 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
One pale spiral shell lies on smooth wet sand near the lower center of the frame. A thin edge of seawater enters from the top, surrounds the shell, then retreats, leaving a darker wet outline. Keep the camera low and locked. The shell stays in place, with a soft reflection appearing briefly under the shallow water. Morning light remains cool and even. Hear one gentle wash and the retreating water. No large wave, additional shells, people, text, speech, or music.
```

**Adjust:** shell color, camera height. **Review:** water wraps around shell geometry; retreat reveals the same sand and shell.
