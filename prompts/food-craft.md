## K055 — The pear fan

**Use:** a food styling insert. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 8 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
Five already-cut pear slices lie stacked on a pale ceramic plate. One adult hand uses two fingertips to spread them into a shallow fan, keeping their bases overlapping. Shoot from a fixed overhead angle with soft side light showing the moist cut surfaces. After the hand leaves, make a tiny focus shift toward the front slice and hold. Hear a quiet ceramic touch and soft fruit movement. Keep all five slices visible and the plate stationary. No knife, additional ingredients, readable text, voiceover, or music.
```

**Adjust:** plate color, pear variety. **Review:** five slices remain; overlaps change through sliding; fruit texture stays consistent.

## K056 — Tea settling in the cup

**Use:** a calm beverage close-up. **Mode:** image to video. **Brief:** 8 seconds, 1:1. **Inputs:** first frame of tea swirling gently inside a plain cup.

```text
Continue the gentle swirl visible in the supplied cup. The amber tea rotates more slowly, leaving small ripples against the inner wall before becoming still. Keep the cup, handle, saucer, and camera fixed. A thin ribbon of steam rises and bends slightly toward the window. Soft reflected light travels across the surface as the motion decays. Hear quiet room ambience and a distant bird outside. Finish on a clear reflection in the settled tea. No pouring, new objects, text, dialogue, or music.
```

**Adjust:** cup glaze, light warmth. **Review:** liquid level stays constant; rotation decays; handle shape remains unchanged.

## K057 — The dough imprint

**Use:** a tactile baking detail. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 10 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
One small oval of dough rests on a lightly floured board. An adult hand presses a plain wooden stamp straight down into its center, pauses, and lifts it vertically to reveal a simple spiral impression. Keep the camera close at a shallow overhead angle so contact and release are visible. The dough compresses slightly and stays attached to the board. Hear a soft press and flour brushing wood. Warm diffuse light shows the imprint without harsh shadows. No readable letters, cutting tools, speech, subtitles, or music.
```

**Adjust:** stamp pattern, board tone. **Review:** impression matches stamp; dough volume changes plausibly; stamp lifts cleanly.

## K058 — A woven edge

**Use:** textile craftsmanship. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 12 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
Close on an adult weaver's hands and a small wooden frame loom. One hand passes a blunt shuttle carrying rust-colored yarn through the existing pale warp threads. The other hand draws it through and gently presses the new row into place with a wooden comb. Keep the camera locked and the loom fully supported on the table. Show the yarn as one continuous strand with slight slack before tightening. Hear wood taps and fiber movement. End on the newly aligned row. No text, dialogue, music, or rapid montage.
```

**Adjust:** yarn colors, weave density. **Review:** shuttle passes through a real gap; thread remains continuous; row forms at the edge.

## K059 — The ink pad print

**Use:** a printmaking process detail. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 10 seconds, 1:1. **Inputs:** none.

```text
An adult printmaker holds a small rubber stamp with a simple leaf design over blank cream paper. They press it onto a dark green ink pad, move it to the paper, press once, and lift to reveal one leaf print. Film overhead with the ink pad on the left and paper on the right. Keep the stamp's rotation consistent between inking and printing. Hear two soft presses and a paper rustle. The print appears only where the stamp touches. No letters, extra impressions, dialogue, or music.
```

**Adjust:** ink color, simple stamp motif. **Review:** ink precedes print; one impression; orientation matches the tool.

## K060 — Clay coil join

**Use:** a ceramics hand-detail shot. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 12 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
On a pottery board, two ends of a soft clay coil almost meet in a ring. Adult fingertips bring the ends together and smooth the join with three small strokes. Stay close at a low three-quarter angle, showing the circular opening and the working fingertips. Keep the clay matte, damp, and slightly uneven. The camera slowly moves closer after the ends touch. Hear soft clay friction and room tone. Finish with the ring intact and hands resting beside it. No spinning wheel, text, dialogue, or music.
```

**Adjust:** clay color, ring diameter. **Review:** seam disappears through smoothing; ring opening stays visible; fingers do not merge with clay.

## K061 — Citrus peel curl

**Use:** a food texture study. **Mode:** image to video. **Brief:** 8 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** first frame of one already-cut citrus peel strip on a plate.

```text
Animate the supplied peel strip as an adult hand lifts one end and gently curls it into a loose spiral. Keep the pale pith on the inside and the colored outer skin facing out. The hand releases the curl onto the same plate, where it relaxes slightly but keeps its shape. Use a fixed macro view with soft daylight. Hear the peel brushing ceramic. Preserve the strip's length, width, surface pores, and single continuous shape. No cutting, juice splash, additional garnish, words, or music.
```

**Adjust:** citrus type, plate color. **Review:** peel bends without splitting; pith orientation stays correct; length does not increase.

## K062 — Brush across paper grain

**Use:** a calligraphy-material visual without text. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 8 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
A broad wet brush touches rough white paper and draws one dark blue curved stroke from lower left to upper right. Frame only the adult hand, brush, and paper. Follow the brush with a small lateral camera move, then hold as pigment spreads subtly into the paper fibers. The brush lifts at the end, leaving a tapered finish. Hear bristles against paper and a quiet water drip beyond the frame. Keep the mark abstract rather than forming a letter. No additional strokes, captions, speech, or music.
```

**Adjust:** pigment color, paper grain. **Review:** mark follows bristles; stroke widens with pressure; pigment spread remains local.

## K063 — The folded napkin pocket

**Use:** a table-setting detail. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 10 seconds, 9:16. **Inputs:** none.

```text
A partly folded linen napkin lies on a plain table. Two adult hands tuck its lower flap into an existing pocket and smooth the top edge. They place one wooden spoon into the pocket, handle pointing upward, then withdraw. Use a fixed overhead view with generous empty space above the spoon. Keep the linen weave visible and its folds physically layered. Hear fabric rubbing and a light wooden contact. The napkin remains one continuous piece with the spoon partly enclosed. No text, speech, music, or extra utensils.
```

**Adjust:** linen color, utensil finish. **Review:** flap enters the pocket; spoon is supported by fabric; folds remain consistent.
