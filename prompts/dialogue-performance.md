## K028 — The empty frame

**Use:** two-person comedy. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 12 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
Two adult gallery visitors stand beside an empty picture frame on a white wall. The visitor on the left, wearing a brown jacket, studies it and says, "I like the space." After a one-second pause, the visitor on the right in a blue sweater replies, "It is mostly space." Both continue looking at the frame. Keep a fixed medium two-shot with the frame between their eyelines. Use diffuse gallery lighting and restrained expressions. The left visitor speaks first; the right visitor's mouth stays closed until the reply. Hear clear speech and soft room ambience. No subtitles, music, or new painting appearing.
```

**Adjust:** wardrobe, frame style. **Review:** line ownership and pauses; same two faces; frame stays empty.

## K029 — A careful introduction

**Use:** a natural presenter opening. **Mode:** image to video. **Brief:** 10 seconds, 9:16. **Inputs:** first-frame portrait of an authorized adult presenter at a desk.

```text
Animate the supplied presenter portrait. Preserve their face, hairstyle, clothing, and desk arrangement. The presenter looks briefly at a plain card on the desk, looks into the lens, and says warmly, "Let me show you the small detail that makes this work." They make one open-palmed gesture toward the empty area on their left, then settle their hands on the desk. Keep a fixed medium close-up and natural blinking. Speech is clear with quiet room tone. The card remains unreadable and stationary. No subtitles, background music, or camera cut.
```

**Adjust:** exact introductory line, gesture side. **Review:** mouth follows speech; gaze returns to lens; hands end in a stable resting position.

## K030 — The bilingual postcard

**Use:** a short language exchange. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 15 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
Two adults sit opposite each other at a small café table with one blank postcard between them. The person on the left says in Spanish, "¿La enviamos hoy?" The person on the right looks at the card, then answers in English, "Yes. Before we change our minds." The left person slides the card toward the right person, who places it in a plain envelope. Use a stable medium two-shot, with warm window light and both hands visible. Keep a short silence between the lines and natural conversational timing. Background café voices remain indistinct. No subtitles or music.
```

**Adjust:** clothing and table material; translate the two lines intentionally. **Review:** each language belongs to the correct speaker; card remains one object.

## K031 — Listening before answering

**Use:** reaction-shot acting exercise. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 10 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
An adult ceramic artist sits at a workbench, shown in a locked close-up. An off-camera adult asks, "Would you make it again?" The artist looks down at a small cracked bowl just visible at the bottom of the frame, runs one thumb along its intact rim, and takes a quiet breath. After a clear pause, they look toward the questioner and answer, "A little differently." Keep the response thoughtful rather than tearful. Soft side light reveals skin and clay texture. Hear the two voices, a faint thumb movement, and room tone. No score or subtitles.
```

**Adjust:** craft object, exact question. **Review:** the visible speaker stays silent during the question; thumb follows the rim.

## K032 — Three people, one route

**Use:** three-speaker blocking. **Mode:** Omni Reference. **Brief:** 15 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** three authorized adult portrait references.

```text
@Image1 defines the adult on the left in a mustard jacket. @Image2 defines the adult in the center in a gray shirt. @Image3 defines the adult on the right in a navy coat. Keep their faces and clothing distinct. They stand at a park path junction in a fixed medium-wide view. The left person points left and says, "This way?" The center person checks the two paths and says, "The quieter one." The right person looks right, nods, and says, "Then over here." They all turn toward the right path but remain in frame. Give each line a separate turn. Hear birds and soft footsteps. No subtitles or music.
```

**Adjust:** setting and clothing references. **Review:** exactly three identities; the left person first points left, then all three turn toward the right path while staying in frame; voices do not swap.

## K033 — A Japanese thank-you

**Use:** a brief service interaction. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 10 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
At a small pottery shop counter, an adult clerk places a wrapped cup into an adult customer's open tote. Frame both people from waist up in one steady shot. The customer looks at the clerk and says in Japanese, "ありがとうございます。大切に使います。" The clerk smiles and gives a small nod after the sentence ends. Keep the tote opening visible until the wrapped cup is fully inside. Use soft afternoon window light. Hear wrapping paper, cloth, and the customer's clear voice. The clerk remains silent. No subtitles, background music, or extra objects entering the bag.
```

**Adjust:** clothing, wrapping color. **Review:** Japanese pronunciation and lip sync; item transfer; clerk waits before nodding.

## K034 — The rehearsal count-in

**Use:** music-performance preparation. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 12 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
Two adult percussionists sit facing each other with one small hand drum each. The drummer on the left says, "Ready? One, two." On the next beat, both tap their drums once together, listen to the resonance, and laugh softly. Keep a medium-wide locked camera that shows faces and both drumheads. Warm rehearsal-room light stays constant. Synchronize each audible impact with visible hand contact. The count-in is spoken rather than sung, and the single tap is followed by silence before the laughter. No backing track, subtitles, additional performers, or repeated drumming.
```

**Adjust:** drum types, room. **Review:** two impacts coincide; hands rebound; laughter occurs after the resonance.

## K035 — The voice behind the curtain

**Use:** voice-reference acting practice. **Mode:** Omni Reference. **Brief:** 12 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** authorized adult portrait and authorized voice profile.

```text
@Image1 defines the adult performer in a plain rehearsal room. Use the supplied voice profile only for this performer's voice. The performer stands beside a heavy blue curtain, listens toward the room behind it, and says quietly, "I am here when you are ready." They then hold the curtain edge aside just enough to reveal a warm strip of light, keeping the space beyond unseen. Use a slow push toward a medium close-up. Preserve the performer's face, clothing, and restrained delivery. Sound: the assigned voice, cloth movement, and room ambience. No other speech, subtitles, or music.
```

**Adjust:** curtain color, delivery. **Review:** voice profile is assigned to one person; curtain moves from hand contact; light comes from behind.

## K036 — The delayed smile

**Use:** silent micro-expression study. **Mode:** image to video. **Brief:** 8 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** authorized adult first-frame portrait.

```text
Hold the supplied portrait as a continuous eight-second close-up. The adult listens to someone outside the frame without speaking. Their eyes shift slightly toward that person, their brow relaxes, and only then does a small asymmetric smile appear. Keep their head nearly still, with one natural blink before the smile. Maintain the original skin texture, hairline, clothing, light direction, and background. The camera stays fixed. Hear only faint indoor ambience and a soft breath. End with the expression held naturally for two seconds. No speech, subtitles, tears, or music.
```

**Adjust:** eyeline direction, emotional intensity. **Review:** expression develops gradually; face proportions remain stable; no unintended mouth movements.
