Material choices are part of the story. These scenes use original characters and simple, visible transformations.

## K046 — The cork gardener

**Use:** a handmade miniature story. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 12 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
A tiny original gardener made from cork and wire stands beside a thimble-sized clay pot on a craft table. In tactile stop-motion, the gardener scoops a little soil with a wooden spoon, presses one round seed into it, and carefully pats the surface. Their cork body tilts with each effort while the wire arms keep their shape. Use a fixed low macro view and warm workshop light. End as the gardener sits beside the pot to wait; the seed stays underground. Hear tiny scraping and tapping sounds. No dialogue, text, or music.
```

**Adjust:** pot color, gardener hat. **Review:** seed count one; spoon stays rigid; soil displacement follows contact.

```text
A row of five paper houses stands on a dark blue cardboard base. As dusk deepens, a warm light appears inside each house from left to right, with a short pause between houses. No text, speech, or music.
```

## K049 — Thread river crossing

**Use:** textile-world animation. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 15 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
On a woven miniature landscape, one original felt beetle approaches a blue thread river. It pulls a loose brown felt strip across the gap, tests the strip with one front leg, and walks over to the opposite bank. Film from a fixed three-quarter overhead angle. Keep the river as layered blue threads that ripple gently, and preserve the beetle's stitched shell and six legs. Finish with the beetle turning back toward the bridge. Hear soft fabric scuffs and quiet water-like rustling. No speech, written text, music, or extra beetles.
```

**Adjust:** felt colors, bridge width. **Review:** bridge reaches both banks; six legs stay distinct; beetle weight gently depresses the strip.

## K051 — Tin bird bath

**Use:** a playful mechanical character. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 12 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** none.

```text
An original tiny bird made from blue tin plates stands beside a shallow ceramic saucer. It lowers one articulated foot into the water, hesitates, then steps in with both feet and gives one short wing shake. Keep the metal plates rigid and the joints visible. Use a low close camera with a gentle sideways move. Water ripples begin at each foot and spread across the saucer; a few small droplets rise during the shake. Hear tin clicks and light splashes. End with the bird still in the water. No flight, words, or music.
```

**Adjust:** plate color, saucer glaze. **Review:** hinges articulate; ripples follow contact; water stays in the saucer.

## K052 — A shadow folds itself

**Use:** a graphic fantasy interlude. **Mode:** text to video. **Brief:** 10 seconds, 1:1. **Inputs:** none.

```text
On a cream paper background, a flat charcoal rectangle casts a darker rectangular shadow to the right. The shadow slowly folds at two clear creases into a small boat silhouette while the physical rectangle stays flat. The shadow boat then slides a short distance across the paper and stops beside its source. Keep the view locked overhead, with a consistent light direction and visible paper grain. Treat the impossible movement as clean graphic animation, preserving sharp edges. Hear faint paper folding sounds. No text, people, speech, or music.
```

**Adjust:** paper and charcoal colors. **Review:** physical rectangle stays unchanged; transformation happens only in the shadow.

## K054 — Mosaic fish turn

**Use:** a decorative animation loop concept. **Mode:** image to video. **Brief:** 8 seconds, 1:1. **Inputs:** first frame of one original mosaic fish against a blue tile background.

```text
Animate only the fish in the supplied mosaic image. Its tile body bends gently as it turns clockwise in a small circle, then returns to its starting orientation. Keep the individual colored tiles recognizable and attached to the same body regions. The background grout grid stays fixed. Use a locked frontal camera and diffuse light that catches the tile glaze. Tiny bubble-like highlights rise behind the tail and fade. Hear soft underwater ambience without music or speech. End with the fish's eye and tail aligned to the first-frame composition.
```

**Adjust:** tile colors, turn speed. **Review:** background stays still; tiles do not detach; ending pose matches the opening.
