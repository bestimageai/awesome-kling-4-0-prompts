## K010 — The magnetic bookmark

**Use:** stationery detail film. **Mode:** image to video. **Brief:** 8 seconds, 1:1. **Inputs:** first frame of an open book, a copper-colored magnetic bookmark, and one adult hand.

```text
Animate this tabletop composition for eight seconds. Preserve the bookmark's rounded rectangle shape and brushed copper finish. The hand lifts its top flap, folds it over one page, and releases it so the two halves meet through the paper. Follow the fingertips with a very small forward camera move, then hold the closed bookmark for two seconds. Soft side light reveals the metal grain and paper fibers. Sound: a page rustle and one quiet magnetic click. Keep the book resting flat, the page count stable, and the contact point visible. No added writing, subtitles, or music.
```

**Adjust:** metal finish, paper tone. **Review:** flap hinges instead of stretching; the page passes between the halves.
