## K091 — From loose ribbon to wrapped gift

**Use:** a controlled product endpoint. **Mode:** first and last frames. **Brief:** 12 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** matching images of a box with loose ribbon and the same box with a tied bow.

```text
Connect the supplied opening and closing images in a continuous tabletop shot. In the opening, the ribbon lies around the same closed box with its ends loose. Two adult hands bring the ends together, form a simple bow, and withdraw. Match the closing image's bow placement, box position, and ribbon length. Keep the camera, tabletop, light, and box dimensions fixed. The ribbon bends and slides through the hands without changing color. Hear fabric and paper contact. Reserve the last two seconds for the completed gift. No speech, subtitles, or music.
```

**Adjust:** ribbon and box colors in both images. **Review:** endpoints match; bow forms through visible movement; hands leave before final hold.

## K092 — Closed fan to open fan

**Use:** an accessory reveal. **Mode:** first and last frames. **Brief:** 8 seconds, 1:1. **Inputs:** same fan, hand, and background in closed and open poses.

```text
Begin with the supplied closed fan image and end with the supplied open fan image. The adult hand rotates the outer rib around the fan's fixed pivot, gradually spreading the paper folds into a semicircle. Keep the pivot in the same location and preserve the painted design as it unfolds. Use the same close framing and soft light throughout. Hear paper folds and a faint wooden click at full opening. Ease to a stop and hold the final open shape for two seconds. No cuts, text, dialogue, or music.
```

**Adjust:** fan artwork and backdrop together. **Review:** ribs share one pivot; design unfolds rather than changes; hand remains attached to grip.

## K093 — The lit window destination

**Use:** a deliberate camera endpoint. **Mode:** first and last frames. **Brief:** 12 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** wide and close images of the same exterior window.

```text
Travel from the wide opening image to the close closing image of the same warmly lit window. Move forward slowly along the visible garden path, maintaining eye level and a continuous perspective. Leaves at the path edges pass naturally through the foreground. The window frame, curtains, wall texture, and interior light remain unchanged. Decelerate before reaching the final composition and hold for two seconds. Hear soft footsteps, leaves, and evening ambience. The camera remains outside the house. No people, cuts, captions, speech, or music.
```

**Adjust:** compatible wide and close compositions. **Review:** approach follows path; window proportions change by perspective; endpoint does not dissolve in.

## K094 — A paper form becomes dimensional

**Use:** a graphic-to-object transition. **Mode:** first and last frames. **Brief:** 10 seconds, 1:1. **Inputs:** flat paper cutout and folded version with matching outline and color.

```text
Animate the flat paper cutout in the opening image into the folded paper object shown in the closing image. Keep the center of the sheet fixed on the same tabletop. Raise each existing flap along a visible crease, one after another, until the final shape stands on its base. Preserve paper color, thickness, and edge count. Use a fixed three-quarter view so the folds are readable. Hear quiet paper creases. Hold the completed object for two seconds. No added paper, hands, text, speech, or music.
```

**Adjust:** fold design with feasible geometry. **Review:** folds follow prepared creases; material remains continuous; final base touches table.

## K095 — Three poses at the window

**Use:** a short portrait sequence. **Mode:** multiple keyframes. **Brief:** 15 seconds, 9:16. **Inputs:** three images of one authorized adult in the same room: facing window, turning, facing camera.

```text
Use the three supplied images in order. For the first five seconds, the adult stands facing the window and lifts their gaze slightly. Over the next five seconds, they turn slowly toward the camera, reaching the middle image's three-quarter pose. During the final five seconds, complete the turn to the last image and hold a relaxed expression. Keep one continuous camera position, the same clothing, facial features, room, and light. Let hair and sleeves settle after the turn. Hear room ambience and soft clothing movement. No dialogue, subtitles, or music.
```

**Adjust:** compatible poses and wardrobe across images. **Review:** turn direction consistent; identity stable; three images are milestones rather than separate people.

## K096 — A table prepared in stages

**Use:** a three-beat hospitality sequence. **Mode:** multiple keyframes. **Brief:** 15 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** same table empty, with a cloth, then with two cups.

```text
Follow the three prepared table images in order. Begin with the empty table. During the first five seconds, two adult hands lay the folded cloth at the center and smooth it to match the middle image. During the next five seconds, place the two cups one at a time onto the cloth in their final positions. Withdraw both hands and hold the last image's arrangement for five seconds. Keep the camera and light fixed. Hear cloth and two separate ceramic taps. No additional tableware, captions, dialogue, or music.
```

**Adjust:** table, cloth, and cup colors consistently. **Review:** objects arrive through hands; two cups remain; cloth does not shift between placements.

## K097 — Pull back to the full artwork

**Use:** a final-frame composition reveal. **Mode:** last-frame direction. **Brief:** 12 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** a final image of an original framed abstract artwork on a wall.

```text
Use the supplied image as the exact final composition. Begin close on one textured blue region of the artwork. Pull the camera steadily backward to reveal its surrounding shapes, then the full frame and wall. Preserve the artwork's colors, marks, proportions, and frame throughout the move. Slow smoothly into the supplied final view and hold for two seconds. Keep the wall lighting unchanged and the art stationary. Hear quiet gallery room tone. No people, newly painted marks, captions, speech, or music.
```

**Adjust:** owned artwork and final framing. **Review:** details belong to final art; pullback reveals rather than morphs; full frame remains uncropped.

## K098 — A tiny stage unfolds

**Use:** miniature set reveal. **Mode:** first and last frames. **Brief:** 12 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** closed cardboard theater and same theater opened.

```text
Connect the supplied closed and open cardboard theater images. The two side panels swing outward around their existing folds, revealing the miniature stage inside. Then the top canopy lifts into its final position. Keep the base stationary and preserve all painted decorations on their original panels. Use a fixed frontal camera with soft side light that makes the paper depth visible. Hear cardboard folds and light taps as panels reach their stops. End on the supplied open arrangement. No characters appearing, text overlays, dialogue, or music.
```

**Adjust:** theater paint and interior set. **Review:** hinges remain fixed; panels preserve area; canopy moves after side panels.

## K099 — A falling leaf's final rest

**Use:** a precise nature endpoint. **Mode:** first and last frames. **Brief:** 10 seconds, 16:9. **Inputs:** same garden scene with leaf on branch and leaf on stone below.

```text
Begin at the supplied opening image. The single amber leaf loosens from the branch, rotates gently as it falls, and comes to rest on the stone in the closing image. Keep the branch, stone, surrounding plants, and camera fixed. The leaf follows a continuous path and turns only a few times, with its stem and veins remaining recognizable. Sound: soft breeze and a faint leaf contact. Hold the final position for two seconds. Preserve the light and composition of the prepared images. No extra falling leaves, text, dialogue, or music.
```

**Adjust:** leaf and garden palette in both images. **Review:** one leaf travels between endpoints; final orientation matches; fall remains continuous.
